# Geographic SEO Review — October 26, 2026

Source: Google Search Console, Web search, trailing three months (July 25–October 24, 2026). Site totals were 18,321 clicks, 1,675,754 impressions, 1.1% CTR, and average position 5.2.

The United States supplies about 79% of clicks and 64% of impressions. English-speaking international markets already contribute meaningful demand without country-specific pages. Spanish-speaking markets have first-page visibility but materially lower CTR, so the immediate opportunity is better intent matching and conversion—not a large program of thin country pages.

Colombia is discoverable, contrary to the impression that OnTimer simply does not appear there: it recorded 4,587 impressions and 14 clicks. The problem is weak engagement at 0.3% CTR. The Colombia query sample is fragmented, but it includes high-ranking Spanish leave-time intent (for example, “a qué hora tengo que salir”) and a small amount of El Dorado airport intent. That is not enough evidence for a Colombia-specific landing-page cluster.

## Recommendations

1. Measure conversions by country and language before expanding localized content. Search impressions alone do not establish app-install or calculator value.
2. Improve the existing Spanish calculator snippets and internal discovery around direct leave-time intent; compare Spain, Mexico, Colombia, and Chile after four weeks rather than creating country variants now.
3. Treat international airport demand as airport/entity demand, not country-page demand. Add a reviewed airport page only when query volume, calculator usage, and destination coverage support it.
4. Keep the place-search experience internationally neutral. The October 26 change removes the geocode-only restriction, includes landmarks and businesses, and asks Google for results in the active page language without imposing a U.S. location restriction.
5. Recheck the country report after the search and location-label changes have enough exposure. Watch CTR and completed-calculation rate separately; the product fix can improve completion without moving rankings.

## Decision

No broad geographic SEO expansion is justified yet. Prioritize Spanish intent alignment and instrumentation, then use country-level conversion evidence to choose the next airport or localized page cohort.
