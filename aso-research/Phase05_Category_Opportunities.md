## Why "Calendar Alarm" Is the Leading Candidate Cluster

| Evidence type | Finding | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | OnTimer's core differentiator is a real alarm before calendar events. | Repository AGENTS.md and [OnTimer listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ontimer-never-be-late/id6755317601) |
| Keyword specificity | "Calendar alarm" describes the mechanism directly; it is not a generic planner or calendar query. | Analyst interpretation from Phase 1 keyword universe |
| Competition signal | One direct competitor found with "Calendar Alarm" in title: Unmissable Calendar Alarm. | [Unmissable listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/unmissable-calendar-alarm/id6752518796) |
| Crowding contrast | Broad calendar/planner categories are occupied by mature apps with broad propositions and high rating counts. | [Fantastical](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fantastical-calendar/id718043190), [Google Calendar](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-calendar-get-organized/id909319292), [Structured](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner/id1499198946), [TickTick](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ticktick-to-do-list-calendar/id626144601), [Any.do](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/any-do-to-do-list-planner/id497328576), [TimeTree](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timetree-shared-calendar/id952578473), [Calendars](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calendars-schedule-planner/id608834326), [Outlook](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/microsoft-outlook/id951937596) |

## Weak Competition Indicators to Verify

| Cluster | Weakness hypothesis | Manual validation needed |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar alarm | Few direct apps; screenshots may not clearly explain alarm-before-event behavior. | Search "calendar alarm"; record top 10 apps, ratings, titles, screenshots. |
| Meeting alarm | Competitors may be meeting schedulers rather than alarm tools. | Search "meeting alarm" and "meeting reminder alarm." |
| Appointment alarm | Results may skew toward business appointment booking, not personal alarms. | Search "appointment alarm" and classify intent mismatch. |
| Persistent reminder | Due already strongly explains repeated reminders, so OnTimer's edge would need calendar-event specificity. | Search "persistent reminder" and "reminder alarm." |
| Time to leave | Results may skew toward maps/traffic, creating feature expectations OnTimer may not satisfy. | Search "time to leave" and "leave reminder." |
