## Strongest Positioning Opportunities

| Opportunity | Fact base | Analyst interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Own the "calendar alarm" mechanism | OnTimer's public product concept and Unmissable's direct title both validate the phrase as market language. [OnTimer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ontimer-never-be-late/id6755317601), [Unmissable](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/unmissable-calendar-alarm/id6752518796) | This is the cleanest bridge between user problem and product behavior. |
| Contrast against passive calendar reminders | Repository positioning says notification blindness is the core problem; broad calendar apps position around organization, not hard-to-ignore alerts. | This distinction may matter more than generic "never late" language. |
| Use work/meeting specificity | Meeting-related lateness is concrete and urgent. | "Meeting alarm" may be a high-intent subcluster if search results are weak. |
| Avoid generic productivity keywords | Broad apps dominate to-do, planner, calendar, and organization language. | Competing there risks hiding OnTimer's strongest differentiator. |
