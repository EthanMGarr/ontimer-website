## Keyword Cluster Fit

| Cluster | Search intent | Best-fit competitor types | OnTimer product fit | Competition quality | Analyst note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calendar alarm | User wants a calendar event to trigger an alarm. | OnTimer, Unmissable | Very high | Low-to-medium known direct competition | Highest conceptual fit discovered so far. |
| Meeting alarm | User wants stronger pre-meeting prompting. | OnTimer, calendar apps, planners | Very high | Medium; generic meeting-reminder apps need manual search | Likely strong intent because it names a common high-stakes use case. |
| Appointment alarm | User wants reminders before appointments. | OnTimer, reminder apps, scheduling apps | High | Unknown until manual search | Could broaden beyond work without becoming generic productivity. |
| Time to leave | User wants departure cue. | Calendar apps, maps apps, travel tools, OnTimer | Medium-to-high | Medium-to-high | Strong problem fit, but traffic/ETA expectations may exceed OnTimer's core scope. |
| Persistent reminder | User wants hard-to-ignore alerts. | Due, alarm apps, reminder apps, OnTimer | High | Medium | Due is a credible competitor here because its listing explicitly emphasizes repeated Auto Snooze reminders. |
| ADHD reminder / time blindness | User wants help with ignored reminders or transitions. | ADHD tools, planners, OnTimer | Medium-to-high | Unknown | Opportunity exists, but messaging must avoid medical overclaiming. |
| Generic calendar | User wants a calendar app. | Fantastical, Google Calendar, TimeTree | Low | Very high | Poor strategic fit because OnTimer is not a calendar app. |
| Generic to-do / planner | User wants productivity suite. | TickTick, Any.do, Structured | Low | Very high | Crowded and mismatched to product positioning. |
