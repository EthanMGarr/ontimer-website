## Description

You glance at your calendar reminder, think "I'll join in 5 minutes," then suddenly realize the meeting started 20 minutes ago.

OnTimer solves this by automatically keeping you on time. Connect your Google, Microsoft, or Apple Calendar once, and OnTimer automatically takes care of the rest.

HOW IT WORKS

OnTimer uses your existing calendar as the source of truth. When your schedule changes, your alarms automatically stay in sync - so you never have to create or maintain manual alarms.

Unlike regular calendar notifications that disappear after a swipe, OnTimer uses persistent alarms that keep reminding you until you dismiss them - helping bridge the gap between noticing a reminder and actually showing up on time.

Use OnTimer for:

- Meetings and calls
- Appointments
- Recurring events
- Work and personal calendars
- Events with locations
- Moments when being late costs time, trust, or stress

Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Microsoft Calendar

OnTimer now supports Apple Calendar, along with Google Calendar and Microsoft Calendar. Connect one calendar for free, or upgrade to cover multiple calendars across work and personal life.

TIME TO LEAVE ALERTS

For events with locations, OnTimer can help you know when to leave. Set your transportation mode, add a buffer, and use Time to Leave alerts to head out at the right moment.

No more guessing when to leave. No more scrambling when traffic is worse than expected. OnTimer helps turn your calendar into the timing layer that gets you moving.

ESPECIALLY HELPFUL IF YOU:

- See calendar reminders and still leave late
- Miss meetings even when alerts are turned on
- Get focused and lose the final few minutes before a call
- Need alarms that keep reminding you until you take action
- Use more than one calendar
- Need help knowing when to join, leave, or switch tasks
- Experience time blindness or notification overload
