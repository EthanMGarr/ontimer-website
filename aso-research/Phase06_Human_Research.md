## Quality Rules

| Rule | Instruction |
|---|---|
| Use a real iPhone or App Store app | Web search results may not match App Store ranking. |
| Use US storefront | This research is US-market oriented. |
| Do not skip low-quality apps | Weak apps are important opportunity evidence. |
| Record exact visible text | Do not paraphrase titles, subtitles, or screenshot headlines. |
| Mark missing data | Use `Not visible`, not a guessed value. |
