# Customer

## Must-Have User

The must-have user is someone who already uses a calendar, already sets reminders, and still misses the moment to act.

They are not disorganized in a generic sense. They often know exactly what is coming. The failure happens in the transition from awareness to movement.

## User Problems

- I saw the reminder and still missed the meeting.
- I meant to leave in five minutes, then got pulled back into work.
- Calendar notifications disappear too quickly.
- My phone has so many notifications that calendar reminders blend in.
- Focus mode, volume, or notification settings make alerts unreliable.
- I use multiple calendars and do not want to create manual alarms.
- My schedule changes, and manual alarms become stale.
- I need help switching modes, not planning my day.
- Some moments cost money, trust, or stress if I am late.

## Emotional Outcomes

- Relief that the important moment will not silently pass.
- Confidence before meetings and appointments.
- Less shame around being late despite caring.
- Less mental load from manually managing alarms.
- Calm from knowing the calendar is covered.
- A sense of control without needing a complicated productivity system.

## Functional Outcomes

- Calendar events become alarms automatically.
- Alerts are harder to miss than standard notifications.
- Alarms require active acknowledgement.
- Multiple calendars can be covered.
- Time To Leave can tell the user when to head out for location-based events.
- The user does not have to duplicate their schedule in another app.

## Customer Language

Use phrases customers would naturally say:

- "I saw the reminder and still forgot to leave."
- "I need something harder to ignore."
- "My calendar reminders are too easy to miss."
- "I do not want to set alarms manually for every meeting."
- "I just need to know when to stop what I am doing."
- "I am not forgetting the meeting. I am missing the moment to move."
- "I use Google Calendar and Outlook."
- "I need an alarm, not another notification."

## Who This Product Is Not For

- People who want a full calendar replacement.
- People looking for a project manager, task manager, or planner.
- People who enjoy manually building reminder workflows.
- People whose main need is shared family scheduling.
- People whose main need is habit tracking.
- People who want medical adherence guarantees.
- People who want coaching, accountability, or behavior scoring.
- People who only need low-stakes notes or casual reminders.
