## Strong Claims Without Overclaiming

“Never be late again” remains the aspirational customer-facing promise. Support it immediately with concrete product behavior: automatic calendar alarms, alerts that stay on screen until dismissed, custom sounds, event-level controls, Early Warning, Time To Leave, or Time To Fly.

Use “harder to miss,” “built to break through the noise,” and “requires acknowledgement” when describing alert performance. Do not turn the aspiration into a technical guarantee. Avoid “impossible to miss,” “can’t miss,” “you can’t ignore,” or any wording that claims OnTimer can guarantee a person will notice or act on an alert.

## Calendar Naming

Lead customer-facing copy with “Outlook Calendar.” Use “Microsoft 365” only when the account or setup context requires the platform name, such as clarifying that Outlook work and school accounts are supported. Do not present Outlook and Microsoft 365 as separate calendar products in feature lists.
